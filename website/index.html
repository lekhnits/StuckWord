Ten seconds of searching looks the same as an instant answer, and nobody notices when the first letter would have been enough.

1 in 3 stroke survivors get aphasia
almost all types of aphasia include word-finding difficulty
at home is where most practice happens, with no therapist to see when someone is stuck

The idea

  1. velmora a word appears
  2. the headband sees “searching”
  3. l_____ still searching after a few seconds: show the first letter

If the searching fades, it waits.
